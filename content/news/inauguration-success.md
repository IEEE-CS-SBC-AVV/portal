## Inauguration Ceremony - A Grand Success

The IEEE Computer Society Student Branch Chapter at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham was officially inaugurated on February 26, 2026. The ceremony was graced by Dr. Arun, IEEE Computer Society Secretary of Madras Region, as the chief guest.

### Event Highlights

- **Chapter Logo Reveal** - The official IEEE CS SBC AVV branding and identity was unveiled
- **Vision & Mission Overview** - Our chapter's goals and objectives for the academic year were presented
- **4-Month Action Plan** - Strategic initiatives and events planned for the upcoming months were announced
- **Leadership Introduction** - The executive committee and core team members were introduced to the attendees
- **Networking Session** - Members connected with fellow IEEE CS enthusiasts

### Looking Ahead

With the inauguration complete, we are now focused on delivering an exciting lineup of events including workshops, hackathons, seminars, and technical competitions. Stay connected for more updates!
